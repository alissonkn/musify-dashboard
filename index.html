How to get your data

  1. Open your Spotify account privacy page and log in.
  2. Scroll to Download your data and tick Extended streaming history. This covers your whole listening history. Account data only includes the last year, but it works here too.
  3. Click Request data, then open the email from Spotify and click its link to confirm the request.
  4. Wait for a second email saying your data is ready. Spotify says this can take up to 30 days, but it often arrives sooner.
  5. Download my_spotify_data.zip from that email and upload it here. You don't need to unzip it.
The download link in Spotify's email expires after a while, so download the file soon after it arrives. The file is read only in your browser and is never uploaded.
  1. Open Google Takeout and log in.
  2. Click Deselect all, then scroll down and tick YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Click All YouTube data included, click Deselect all, tick only history and click OK.
  4. Click Multiple formats and change history from HTML to JSON. This step is required.
  5. Click Next step, keep .zip and Export once, and click Create export. The email usually arrives within minutes.
  6. Download takeout-….zip from the email and upload it here. You don't need to unzip it.
YouTube doesn't record how long you watched, so every video you opened counts as one play. The export only includes history Google still keeps: if auto-delete is on in your YouTube history settings, older history is gone. The file is read only in your browser and is never uploaded.
