How to get your Spotify data

  1. Open your Spotify account privacy page and log in.
  2. Scroll to Download your data and tick Extended streaming history. This covers your whole listening history. Account data only includes the last year, but it works here too.
  3. Click Request data, then open the email from Spotify and click its link to confirm the request.
  4. Wait for a second email saying your data is ready. Spotify says this can take up to 30 days, but it often arrives sooner.
  5. Download my_spotify_data.zip from that email and upload it here. You don't need to unzip it.
The download link in Spotify's email expires after a while, so download the file soon after it arrives. The file is read only in your browser and is never uploaded.
